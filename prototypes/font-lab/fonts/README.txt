Font lab assets.

All 90 built Iosevka Custom .woff2 are here (flat). They are gitignored.
manifest.json lists the curated subset the tester auto-loads on open
(the 5-width ramp in upright + oblique, plus the Thin->Heavy weight ramp).

To auto-load a different set, edit manifest.json. To try any of the other
72 faces ad hoc, just drag the .woff2 from this folder onto the tester.

The build recipe that produced these is preserved at:
  audits/design-additions/2026-06-30-gw-gdm9061-reskin/iosevka-build-plan.toml
