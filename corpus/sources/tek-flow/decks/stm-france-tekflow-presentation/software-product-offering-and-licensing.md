# Software Product Offering + Licensing

## Summary

Closing licensing slide with three-tier pricing: **Per Technology** (all generations of a tech, e.g. PCIe Gen1–Gen6) and **Per Generation** (single generation, e.g. PCIe Gen6 only), each offered as Perpetual / Annual Maintenance / Annual Subscription. Per-Technology Perpetual is **$75,000** + $7,500/yr maintenance, or **$30,000/yr** subscription. Per-Generation Perpetual is **$30,000** + $3,000/yr maintenance, or **$12,000/yr** subscription. The slide also encodes a Wow/Fit scoring rubric: **10 = "willing to buy this today"**, **1 = "doesn't justify the value/$"**.

Speaker notes detail the perpetual-vs-subscription mechanics (12-month included maintenance, lapse penalty of 25% premium + back-maintenance fees, three FAQ answers).

## Speaker notes

### Slide 16

> **Perpetual licenses** give the license to use the software indefinitely, and the first year of annual Maintenance Service is included in the initial purchase price. After 12 months, annual maintenance renewal is needed for updates/maintenance. If your subscription lapses, renewal later shall attract fees for the continuation of subscription, back maintenance fees for the lapsed period, and a **premium fee of 25%** of the annual cost of Software Maintenance Service.
>
> **Annual Subscription** gives the license to use the software for 12 months, and annual Maintenance Service is included in the license fee.
>
> **FAQ:**
> 1. Does the SW include measurement and design tools like DOPJET and SDLA? **Prerequisites remain the same as we currently offered.**
> 2. Are all generations provided for every technology offering? **Depends on the technology selected. If the technologies are still active in the Industry, they will be a part of the offering.**
> 3. What will be available as part of first release? **Latest upcoming technologies will be part of first releases; beta will be available for evaluation subjected to agreement.**

## Confidence notes

- **All prices in USD** (not labeled, but consistent with Tek US-HQ pricing conventions). No EUR price for STM France presented.
- **"DOPJET"** is DPOJET — Tektronix's Phase Noise / Jitter / Eye-Diagram measurement application (the "P" typo in the FAQ is verbatim from the source).
- **"SDLA"** is Tektronix's Serial Data Link Analysis software (signal integrity / channel emulation).
- **The 25% premium fee for lapsed subscriptions** is a commercial lock-in mechanic; preserved here for completeness but is licensing-team policy, not a product feature.
