# Empathy Map — ElectroRent (HSS rental builder)

## Summary

Customer immersion notes for an **ElectroRent** persona who builds global rental offerings for HSS technologies. The first **non-end-user** customer in this deck — ElectroRent is a **channel partner** that rents Tek + competitor instruments to end customers. JTBD: stay current on market needs, understand customer rental criteria, build vendor-selection plans for the rental portfolio. Pain points: **not aware of current market movement** and **not sure about future TM-vendor roadmaps**. Direct ask: HSS compliance solutions across various technologies + collaboration on Customer Intelligence.

## Cross-references

- **First channel-partner persona in the corpus.** Unlike the end-user empathy maps (Ampere / Synopsys / Intel), ElectroRent is asking for **strategic roadmap visibility** rather than a workflow / tool gap. Implication for the redesign: rental-channel partners want to be informed of TekExpress / TekFlow roadmap as a sales-enablement input.
- **"S: / E: / F:"** prefixes (Social / Emotional / Functional JTBD per the Christensen JTBD framework) appear explicitly on this slide — the **only** Designcon empathy map to use the prefixes. The Ampere, Synopsys, and Intel slides write JTBD in narrative form. This is the format-lock cue: the deck author Subhasis began with narrative JTBD and converted to S/E/F prefixes by slide 7. The **mAR 2023 ind Customer VOC** deck ([`india-customer-voc-2023-march/`](../../india-customer-voc-2023-march/_index.md)) then uses S/E/F prefixes throughout.
- **"Not sure about future roadmaps of TM vendors"** is a channel-partner pain. The [TekFlow 2022-12 UI/UX Roadmap](../../../tek-flow/decks/tekflow-voc-synthesis-2022-12/ui-ux-roadmap.md) is precisely the kind of artifact ElectroRent is asking for — internal roadmap, not customer-facing. There is a translation gap.
- **No customer-name attendees** are recorded for ElectroRent on the [customer-connects-summary](./customer-connects-summary.md) slide 8 — ElectroRent is not in the Customer Connects table on slide 8. Likely a single floor-conversation captured separately during the booth rotation.
