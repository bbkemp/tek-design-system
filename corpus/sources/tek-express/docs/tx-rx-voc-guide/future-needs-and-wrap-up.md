# 5. Future Needs & Ideal Solution, and Wrap-Up

## Summary

The closing question block. Goal: uncover what an ideal compliance solution looks like — top-3 improvements (dream big), new/emerging capabilities (ML-driven test suggestions, collaboration, hardware CI/CD hooks), must-haves vs. aspects customers would sacrifice, and competitor features admired or pitfalls to avoid. The block ends with the guide's only forced-ranking instrument: a nine-item candidate feature list for future Tx/Rx offerings (Reporting, up-to-2X measurement performance, disaggregated UI with PC throughput up to 4X faster, Simple UX, error handling/root-causing, Multirun, Multilane, offline analysis, others) that the customer ranks 1 = top ask. The Wrap-Up asks for final feedback and signals Tek's intent to return with a proposal for the customer to review.

## Content

**Goal:** *Uncover what an ideal compliance solution looks like*

- **Top 3 Improvements:** If you could pick three improvements to TekExpress (OR CURRENT TOOL) , what would they be? (Feel free to dream big – e.g., "5× faster testing", "one-click test bench setup", "cloud-based results sharing", etc.) Why are these top priorities for you?
- **Innovative Features:** Is there any *new or emerging capability* you wish TekExpress (OR CURRENT TOOL) had (for example, ML-driven test suggestions, built-in collaboration features, hooking into CI/CD flows for hardware)? This is a chance to think beyond what exists today.
- **Must-Haves vs. Nice-to-Haves:** What aspects of TekExpress (OR CURRENT TOOL) (or compliance testing in general) do you consider **must-have essentials** that should not change in a new platform? Conversely, are there any aspects you'd be willing to **sacrifice or change radically** if it led to an overall better solution?
- **Competitor Comparison:** Without naming specific competitors if you prefer, are there features or experiences you've seen in other compliance test tools that you **really liked** (and wish TekExpress had)? Alternatively, are there any pitfalls in other tools that you want TekExpress (OR CURRENT TOOL) to avoid?
- What are the top features that you think is the big NEED in future offerings in Tx and Rx solutions (Ex – Reporting, Performance improvements, Simple UX, Error handling (Root causing), Multirun, Multilane, Offline analysis, Disaggregated UI with PC through put). CAN YOU RANK (1being the top ask)
  - Reporting
  - Performance improvements (Measurement optimization within Scope, Improvement up to 2X)
  - Disaggregated UI with PC through put (Complicates the setup with external PC/Laptop), But gives fastest test time (Up to 4X)
  - Simple UX
  - Error handling (Root causing)
  - Multirun
  - Multilane
  - Offline analysis (Scope is used for ACQUISTION ONLY, Processed later on PC (Ex overnight)
  - Others if any

### Wrap-Up:

- **Final Thoughts:** Do you have any other feedback on TekExpress (OR CURRENT TOOL) or compliance testing that we haven't covered? Any concerns or hopes for our next-gen solution you'd like to share?

Thank you for your time and valuable insights. Your feedback is critical in shaping the next generation of Tektronix compliance software.

We would like to continue our conversation with you and would like to come back with proposal that global industry also share and review our proposal.
