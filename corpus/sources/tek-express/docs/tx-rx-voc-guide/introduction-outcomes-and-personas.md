# Introduction script, expected outcomes, and target personas

## Summary

The verbatim interview opening the interviewer speaks to the customer (marked TALK ONLY — not to be shared over email or other digital sources), framing the interview as validation of current gaps, future needs, and competitive positioning of TekExpress Software over ~60 minutes covering workflow, pain points, and ideal solution. Followed by two internal-only definitions: the expected outcomes of the VOC round (pain points, quantified UI/UX feedback, parity vs. competing tools, differentiation opportunities, prioritized feature backlog) and the five target personas to recruit (signal integrity/validation engineers, compliance and interoperability engineers, technical leads/architects, lab/test automation owners, engineering/lab managers).

## Content

### Introduction: (TALK ONLY) — NOT TO BE SHARED OVER EMAIL OR OTHER DIGITAL SOURCES.

Hello (Customer), this is (Interviewer). I have several other team members on the line with me today.

**Introduction:** The purpose of this interview is to validate current gaps, future needs, and competitive positioning of **TekExpress Software**. We will ask questions about your testing workflow, pain points, needs, and preferences – covering compliance, beyond compliance, and general testing challenges. Your feedback will help us improve our offerings and better meet your expectations.

During our conversation, we'll cover topics such as **workflow**, **pain points**, and what an **ideal solution** looks like. This interview should take ~60 minutes. Please feel free to ask questions and share your honest opinions. We value your input, and responses will remain confidential.

### (INTERNAL TO TEKTRONIX ONLY - UNDERSTADING OF THE OUTCOME)

**Expected Outcomes:** Clear articulation of customer pain points and improvement areas; quantified feedback on UI/UX and workflow efficiency; explicit points of parity vs. competing tools; differentiation opportunities for TekExpress; prioritized feature-level backlog aligned to customer needs.

**Target Personas:**

- Signal Integrity / Validation Engineers (primary TekExpress OR OTHER COMPLINACE APP users).
- Compliance & Interoperability Engineers.
- Technical Leads / Architects.
- Lab / Test Automation Owners.
- Engineering Managers/Lab Managers (influencers/decision makers for tools).
