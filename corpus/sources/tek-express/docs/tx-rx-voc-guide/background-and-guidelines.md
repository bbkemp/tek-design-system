# Background and Guidelines (INTERNAL TO TEKTRONIX)

## Summary

Subhasis Bera's cover letter to Tek Sales Leaders framing why this VoC round exists — a change in Platform strategy for the High-speed Transmitter and Receiver (Tx/Rx) Compliance and Conformation Platform requires validating the current and future landscape of customer jobs-to-be-done (JTBD), testing acceptance of the latest industry solutions (QualiPHY2, AGGC, CLARIUS, XR8 Tx), and removing biases carried from the previous VOC done around 2020. Followed by three critical engagement guidelines: check NDA status before open competitive conversation, keep questions open-ended when no NDA exists, and keep the customer connection live for post-VOC summary review.

## Content

### Background (INTERNAL TO TEKTRONIX)

Hello Sales Leaders,

With changes in Platform strategy for High speed Transmitter and Receiver technologies Compliance and Conformation Platform, it is important that we are able to validate the current and future landscape of customers jobs to be done (JTBD). We would also want to understand the acceptance of the latest solutions offered in the industry which include QualiPHY2, AGGC, CLARIUS & XR8 Tx solutions enhancement. This will help us ensure that we are validating our Hypothesis and assumptions to the current and future needs of the platform. This will help us avoiding any biases that we carry from our previous VOC done around 2020. This will also help us ensure that we are aligned on the customer needs and define the right product for success of Tx/Rx solutions offering for the FUTURE OF TEST. Let us remember that Tektronix was first to release Compliance automation & #1 in Tx/Rx Software offering. Let us work together to get back our leadership position in this space. Talking to your Region's key customers who use Tx and Rx (ANY TOOL) will help us unravel the secrets that is needed to make the best offering for the future.

Thank you for the amazing support.

Best Wishes,
Subhasis Bera
Principal Product Manager (Solutions Platform and Decoders)

### Guidelines (INTERNAL TO TEKTRONIX)

Below critical guidelines for this VOC and post VOC engagement:

1. Recommended to check that we have a continued non-disclosure agreement with the customer who would be helping us with the VOC. This might be needed if we want to have a direct subject matter expert or open conversation on competitive positioning
2. NO NDA : we can continue with the VOC and ensure that the questions used as per the guide below are open ended and not targeted to specifics like "We are doing this VOC to pivot from our plans of offering ETC.
3. Let's us keep the connect Live : We would want to go back to the same set of people at customer who can help us review the summary of the collective VOC or go for reviewing a specific feature where we feel that customer might be adding value.
