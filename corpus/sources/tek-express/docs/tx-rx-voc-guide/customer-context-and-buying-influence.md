# 1. Customer Context & Buying Influence

## Summary

The opening question block of the interview body. Goal: understand the user's role, influence, and work environment — background and tenure, group mission, daily T&M equipment and software tools, purchase decision authority, technology/interconnect responsibility (PCIe, DDR/LPDDR, USB, Ethernet, Automotive), and project-stage engagement (pre-silicon through production). An internal annotation notes this data can be obtained from Sales Leaders rather than spending interview time on it.

## Content

**Goal:** *Understand the user's role, influence, and work environment.*

> **Internal annotation (highlighted in source):** "We can get this Internally from our Sales Leaders"

**Questions:**

- Could you give us a little background information about your work and experience? How long have you been at [Company]? Do you have direct reports?
- What is your group's primary mission?
- What Test & Measurement equipment do you use in your day-to-day work?
- What software tools do you use daily (e.g., TekExpress, competitor tools, in-house tools)?
- Are you responsible for making decisions on what tools or equipment to purchase?
- What is your primary role in validation or compliance testing? Which technologies/interconnects are you responsible for (e.g., **PCIe, DDR/LPDDR, USB, Ethernet, Automotive, etc.**)?
- At which project stages do you typically engage? (Pre-silicon, bring-up, EVT/DVT/PVT, production, etc.)
