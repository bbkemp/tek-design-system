# Cable assembly details

## Summary

Physical and electrical specifications for the supplied electrochemistry interface cable that connects the SMU to a 2-, 3-, or 4-terminal electrochemical test cell. One meter long, shielded, with stackable safety banana plugs on both ends, rated 42 V maximum and 7 A on HI/LO leads.

## Content

The cable is an electrically-shielded 1 m (39.37 in.) assembly with stackable, safety banana plugs on both ends. This cable simplifies the connections between your SMU potentiostat or galvanostat and an electrochemical test cell.

*Figure 4: ECHEM accessory cable dimensions* — Shows the cable with one meter / 39.37 in. length annotated. Four connectors at one end (LO, SLO, SHI, HI) and four at the other end (CE, RE, WE, WES), color-coded.

**Electrical characteristics:**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum voltage (assembly) | 42 V |
| Maximum current (assembly) | 7 A on HI and LO, less than 1 mA on SHI and SLO |
