# Jobs-to-be-Done UX feedback — Garuda VOS Synthesis

## Summary

All 166 Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) entries from the `JTBD_Feedback_UX` sheet of the Garuda VOS Synthesis spreadsheet. Each entry is a verbatim functional JTBD captured from an internal Tektronix SME (Sales / Tech persona), tagged with the UX section it applies to (Tests, Manage, Results, Report, Dashboard, Debug, API, Others) and three numerical attributes (Ranking 1-3, WOW score, FIT score).

Sheet header note: *"JOBS TO BE DONE — High-level motivation/driving force behind an individual's decision-making, the 'why' behind what they do or select. Jobs are immutable and timeless – they have and always do exist, but how we can solve or meet them changes over time through innovation. Jobs change based on the context of a situation."*

## Column inventory

Per-row fields captured: SME ID (`Ex` column), Industry, Persona, Company, Country, Title, Application/DUT, Type of JTBD, Section of Ux, Job-to-be-Done (verbatim text), Ranking (1=Top, 2=Medium, 3=Low priority), WOW score, FIT score, JIRA ID (when linked).

First Name and Last Name columns are present in the source but redacted at chunk boundary per corpus PII policy — individual contact identifiers are stripped; organizational labels (Company, Title, Country, Industry, Persona) are preserved. SME IDs (`Ux-SME-NNN`) are preserved as the linkage key.
